About Us

Our Story

Two people who are happiest with the sea in view. Over the past few years we have collected sunsets, cliff-top walks and far too many selfies — from the cliffs of Madeira to the desert outside Dubai. In March 2025, by the lake in Rome’s Villa Borghese, Victor got down on one knee and Nela said yes. Now we can’t wait to celebrate the next chapter with you, by the sea in Cyprus.

The Setting

Villa Kyma

A private seafront villa on the quiet edge of Coral Bay, where a wide lawn and palm-lined garden run straight down to the Mediterranean. Kyma is Greek for “wave” — fitting for a day spent by the sea.

Photos: Ezoria Villas

  • Seafront garden & lawn

Getting Here

Travel & Stay

Paphos International Airport (PFO) is the nearest airport, around 30–35 minutes’ drive from Villa Kyma. Larnaca International Airport (LCA) is a further option, roughly 1¾–2 hours away by road.

Where to Stay

Coral Bay and Peyia, just a few minutes from the villa, have hotels, apartments and holiday villas to suit most budgets, with Paphos town about 20 minutes further along the coast. [Add recommended hotels once you've picked a shortlist.]
